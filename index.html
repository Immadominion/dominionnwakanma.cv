Dominion Nwakanma

Mobile and product engineer, currently building Trimmy and Chumbucket.

I build apps people use, the open-source SDKs other developers build on, and the courses that teach them. I wrote Solana Mobile Mastery for Blueshift and publish 13 packages on pub.dev.

I convened Hack4FUTO 5.0, my university's student hackathon, led the mobile track at GDSC FUTO, and I'm a Solana AllStars ambassador in Nigeria.

Excited by interfaces that feel fast and human: how a sheet settles, how a list scrolls, what a button says. Software engineering graduate from FUTO, based in Nigeria.
